# Ken Burns and PBS shot log (The American Revolution, episode 1, g5WkOr565I4, 1:53:39)

Method: frame difference at 1 s steps over five windows (0:00 to 15:00, 30:00 to 35:00, 60:00 to 65:00, 90:00 to 95:00, 108:20 to 113:35; 2120 samples), cut threshold 0.18. On this film a 1 s step undercounts: the landscapes are dark and the camera slow, so a run of three or four landscape shots can register as one 60 to 77 s "shot." Counts below are therefore floors; the notes say where a long row is really several shots. Relation codes as elsewhere, plus ART (a period artefact under the camera: painting, engraving, newspaper, letter, map), LAND (present-day landscape or interior), WIT (an interviewee), MAP (a map with animated marks), DOC (a document voice reading, the frame being whatever is under it).

No stock footage, no re-enactment with faces (the re-enactor camp and formation are shown at a distance), no text added to any frame.

Holds: the sampled rows run 4 to 43 s when they are single shots; portraits are held longest (34 s for Grenville, 28 s for young Washington, 32 s for a witness); landscapes under readings run 20 to 40 s; the catalogue sentence at 5:27 to 6:13 cuts every 3 to 5 s on the place names, the fastest cutting in the film outside the explosion and gunfire flashes (0 s rows at 4:58, 6:56, 6:57).

Camera: 82 of 118 rows have measurable motion; on artefacts the move is a push-in or a tilt that reveals, timed to the sentence (the snake, the rights list, the Wheatley frontispiece); on landscapes a drift; on witnesses none. The map at 92:55 is the one animated element, a dotted route drawn as the sentence is spoken.

Sound as picture: the captions' sound cues coincide with pictures that are the sound's source (sawing with sawdust, flames with a forge, waves with surf, drums with a map, a dog with a night lane), so the sound design is literal where the picture is atmospheric.

What the picture never does here: joke, caption, show a modern face other than a witness, show a number or a name as text, or move faster than the sentence.
