- What is kept here: words and numbers only. No frame, clip, logo, thumbnail or caption text is copied into this repo.

## In two sentences

What the channel does and what makes it recognisable.

## Voice

Who is speaking, to whom, in what person and tense; the register (teacher, reporter, comedian, narrator); what they never do.

## Language

Sentence shape, signposting, signature phrases, how terms are defined, how jokes or asides are placed, how sources are cited, how sponsors are handled. Quote at most a phrase or two, with the time.

## Visuals

Ground, ink and accent colours; what fills the frame (presenter, drawings, footage, screenshots, slides, artefacts); type on the frame (how much, how big); camera (static, push, Ken Burns, cuts); how elements enter; what carries continuity (a bug, a wedge, a border pattern).
