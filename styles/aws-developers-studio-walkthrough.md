# Style: AWS Developers (studio walkthrough)

- Video studied: "Amazon Bedrock for Beginners: From First Prompt to AI Agent (Full Tutorial)" (FAgmR9VV0GQ), 44:41, AWS Developers, 65K views at 5 months
- URL: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FAgmR9VV0GQ
- Studied on: 30 Sep 2026, by the video lane chat, with the built-in browser, muted; auto captions fetched; frames viewed at the open, the chapter cards, and four working stretches
- Credits shown: presenter Morgan Willis; chapters in the description; a series card at the end
- What is kept here: words and numbers only. No frame, clip, logo, thumbnail or caption text is copied into this repo.

## In two sentences

A long-form tutorial that opens on a presenter in a warm studio, states the outline as a checklist, then spends each chapter on a screen recording (console, editor, terminal) inside a branded frame with the presenter in a picture-in-picture corner. It is the format most the product walkthroughs would default to if a person were on camera.

## Voice

Second person, instructional, calm and even; "In this video we'll ... together step by step". Each step is announced before it is shown ("From here, I'm going to select..."), and the why is repeated after the how ("because this is where a lot of the real control lives"). No jokes, no hype beyond "powerful", no filler; 44 minutes without a pause over 2 s.

## Language

Opens with the learner's questions (0:13 to 0:49: "How do you know which model to choose? How do you authenticate securely?"), reassures ("you really don't"), then gives the outline as a numbered checklist (1:35 to 2:13) matched by an on-screen checklist. Chapters each run console, then code, then run, then explain, and the last chapter combines everything into one agent. Sentences average 15.9 words, 27 over 30 in 459.

## Visuals

A warm set (desk, laptop, wood panelling, framed prints, a lamp, a plant) for the open and transitions. Chapter cards as a blue rounded rectangle centred over the presenter with a small series label; a three-item checklist with check icons on blue tabs in the left third while the presenter keeps talking on the right. Working frames: the screen recording in a rounded frame on a dark ground with a blue-and-purple geometric border pattern; the presenter in a rounded picture-in-picture about a quarter of the frame width, bottom right or top right. The recording is scaled to fit, so code is small (1.5 to 2 percent of frame height); the cursor is the motion. Camera static on the set with a slight push at the top. Chapter titles about 6 percent of frame height, white sans on blue. Only two accents in the whole video (blue and purple); the console and editor carry their own colours.
