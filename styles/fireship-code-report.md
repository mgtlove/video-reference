# Style: Fireship (The Code Report)

- Video studied: "Did a 50 year old military secret just solve agent prompt injection?" (I_KVMFrUtPk), 5:03, Fireship, 287K views at 4 hours, 4.29M subscribers
- URL: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I_KVMFrUtPk
- Studied on: 30 Sep 2026, by the video lane chat, with the built-in browser, muted; auto captions fetched; frames viewed at about twelve points and a 20 s stretch counted shot by shot
- Credits shown: Fireship (Jeff Delaney) writes and voices; the sponsor is named in the narration at 1:00 and again at 4:45
- What is kept here: words and numbers only. No frame, clip, logo, thumbnail or caption text is copied into this repo.

## In two sentences

A five-minute tech news brief read fast in a deadpan first person over a near-black comp of screenshots, flat icons, stock clips and memes, cut about every three seconds. The format is so fixed (cold open, date stamp, ident, background, the new idea, a demo, a verdict, sign-off) that viewers know where they are at every second.

## Voice

First person, present tense, irreverent and dry. Every two or three sentences a joke, often crude, never explained; analogies are concrete (rooms rated for classification levels); the demo uses an absurd but real running project so the technical claim is testable; the verdict is honest with numbers and caveats (75 versus 96 percent, more tokens, "in preview").

## Language

Run-on sentences chained with "And", "But", "So" (average 19 words, 8 over 30) with no pause over 2 s anywhere. Signature lines mark the structure: "Let's dive in", "It is <date> and you're watching The Code Report" (1:13), "This has been the code report", "I will see you in the next one". The sponsor is introduced as the subject of the story, not as a break. Structure by time: hook 0:00 to 0:35, pivot 0:51, sponsor as subject 1:00, promise and ident 1:13, background as a list of three prior ideas 1:22 to 2:27, the new idea by analogy 2:27 to 3:28, demo 3:28 to 4:13, verdict 4:13 to 4:45, callback and sign-off.

## Visuals

Near-black ground for all graphics. Screenshots (articles, posts, repositories, documentation figures) dropped in slightly rotated with a drop shadow; flat white icons and logos; monospace coloured text for prompts (green for the good one, red for the injection); one multi-colour diagram of three horizontal bands with bold all-caps labels; stock clips for four-second beats; a terminal screenshot; big red arrow annotations; a reaction-face meme beside a code block; an animal clip as a punchline; the ident in outline type on a dark texture with a red stripe. Measured over 20 s: six shots (3.3 s a shot) with in-shot additions (an arrow, a second screenshot) every 2 s. Labels about 6 percent of frame height, monospace text about 3 percent. No camera; every entrance is a quick scale-in or slide-in.
