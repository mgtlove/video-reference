# Style: Casually Explained (doodle)

- Video studied: "Casually Explained: Engineering" (tqcThEqoYmA), 6:13, Casually Explained
- URL: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tqcThEqoYmA
- Studied on: 30 Sep 2026, by the video lane chat, with the built-in browser, muted; manual captions fetched; eight frames viewed across the run
- Credits shown: the creator draws and voices; sponsor Saily, read in the narration at the open and from about 5:00
- What is kept here: words and numbers only. No frame, clip, logo, thumbnail or caption text is copied into this repo.

## In two sentences

A deadpan monotone narrator over a white page of black stick-figure line art, with the odd photograph or stock chart collaged in, cut on the punchline rather than on the clock. The look is one line weight, one accent colour and a lot of white.

## Voice

First person, flat, self-deprecating; the joke is always the last clause of a sentence, delivered without a change of tone. Never signposts a joke, never laughs, never addresses "you guys". The sponsor read is in the same register and is itself part of the comedy.

## Language

Long run-on sentences that keep adding one more absurd qualifier (average 17.7 words, 13 over 30 in 76). Numbers are given with fake precision for comedy. 216 wpm on paper, but the delivery is unhurried: the speed comes from cutting every pause, not from rushing.

## Visuals

Plain white ground; black hand-drawn line art in the manner of xkcd: stick figures with round heads, the host as a round-headed cartoon in a red shirt (the only recurring saturated colour). Thought bubbles that collage real photographs and a stock chart inside a hand-drawn outline; real photographs with a drawn character overlaid, so the drawing and the photo share a frame (a city street with a price written over it in red marker and a cartoon face); an AI-generated painting used as a gag with hand-lettered labels. Text is hand lettered in the same black line, capitals, sparse; labels do the work of captions. Almost no motion: held frames swapped on the beat of the sentence, with a small pop or slide when an element is added. About one frame every 4 to 8 s, timed to the punchline. No lower thirds, no logo, no music bed.

## Pacing, measured

| Measure | Value |
|---|---|
| Words per minute | 216 |
| Sentences, average words, over 30 words | 76, 17.7, 13 |
| Silences over 2 s | none |
| Cut or new element, seconds between | 4 to 8 s, on the last word of a sentence |
| Text on frame, height as a share of 1080 | hand lettering 4 to 6 percent |

## Look pack draft

`styles/looks.json` entry `doodle`. Ground #ffffff; ink #111111; accent red #d9342b; stroke width 4 px, roughness 1.0 seeded, round caps; type a hand-style sans in capitals at 60 px for labels, sentence case at 54 px for anything longer; figures as stick figures with round heads from a small vocabulary (stand, point, sit at a laptop, shrug); photographs and recreated screens inside a drawn frame; entrance pop (scale 0.9 to 1, 180 ms, ease-out) or slide 16 px; cuts every 6 s full, 9 s formal, always on the sentence end; camera static; text on frame as labels; evidence as recreated screens inside a drawn outline.
