# The worked example in the first person

Group: argument. Say it like: "let us say I have", "a concrete example", "read the number out in full", "make it specific".

## Observed

- IBM Technology (bs1qPy_CWkM): "Let's say I have a website" with four things the agent could build; "I don't care if AI changed 1000 lines of code, I care that the right 1000 lines changed"; "I can review five files myself, I can't realistically review 500."
- Stephane Maarek (10JKpg-eqZU): the IP address read aloud in full, twice; made-up domains read aloud ("application1.mypublicdomain.com"); the server voiced ("by the way, in my records it looks like this"), which is the loudest line in the lecture.
- Fireship (I_KVMFrUtPk): the private data is embarrassing on purpose, the attack is the owner asking the agent to be careless, the result is read off the terminal.

## The numbers

Maarek: numbers read in full ("32 dot 45 dot 67 dot 85", "zero point fifty dollars"); IBM: 5 versus 500 files, 1000 lines. Fireship's demo 47 s.

## The effect

A specific number or a voiced character turns a mechanism into a scene the listener can picture; the first person puts the narrator on the learner's side of the desk and makes the example a confession rather than a lecture.

## Counter-example

Ken Burns' narrator never uses the first person and gives numbers precisely but sparingly (2 percent own two-thirds); the specificity is the record's, not the narrator's.

## What it needs

`COPY` values that are real-looking and read aloud exactly as shown; the rule that product output is captured, never invented.
