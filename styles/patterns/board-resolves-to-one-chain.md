# The board resolves into one arrow chain

Group: word-and-picture. Say it like: "draw the structure first", "container then contents", "one diagram per section", "the whole argument on one surface".

## Observed

- IBM Technology (bs1qPy_CWkM): structure is drawn as a container first (a house, a circle, a list heading) and filled second; every board ends as one arrow chain; the hands point at the item being discussed; the camera moves closer for a claim made without writing (4:10, 6:20) and wider when the board matters.

## The numbers

4 boards, 84 framing changes, mean framing 5.2 s, boards held up to 2 minutes; 11 triplets in 64 sentences, each one written as three items; 6 pauses of 2 to 5 s where the longer words are written.

## The effect

The viewer sees the shape of the argument before its parts, and at the end of each movement the board is the summary, so there is nothing to remember except the chain. Writing in silence makes the word's appearance the event.

## Counter-example

Maarek's slide accumulates bullets rather than a chain; Fireship's diagram arrives whole; AWS's diagrams restate the sentence's nouns as boxes after the doing. A board that fills with text but never resolves is a list, not an argument.

## What it needs

Seeded strokes that draw on in order (`drawOn` sequence), a container drawn before its contents, a final arrow that connects them, and a hold with no voice while the longest word draws.
