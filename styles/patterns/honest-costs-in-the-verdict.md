## The numbers

Fireship: 3 costs, 2 with numbers, in 33 s; the verdict is the second-loudest passage (0.214). Maarek: 2 prices, read in full.

## The effect

A verdict that names costs is believed; one that does not is an advertisement. Saying the price before the hands-on means nobody feels tricked later.

## Counter-example

IBM makes no verdict and names no product; How Money Works' verdicts are understated and quiet rather than costed. A costed verdict needs numbers the video can stand behind.

## What it needs

A verdict card scene with the numbers as `COPY`, and the rule that any number shown is sourced.
