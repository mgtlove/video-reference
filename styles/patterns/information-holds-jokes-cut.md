# Information holds, jokes cut

Group: word-and-picture. Say it like: "hold on the diagram", "let the demo sit", "fast in argument, slow in explanation", "how long to hold a frame".

## Observed

- Fireship (I_KVMFrUtPk): the six longest holds (23.5, 19.5, 16.5, 15.5, 12.5, 11.5 s) are all demo output, diagrams or the licence page; jokes run 0.5 to 3 s and never hold.
- Casually Explained (tqcThEqoYmA): two kinds of long hold, the host alone while the voice runs a paragraph, and a punchline image left up 11 to 14.5 s while the voice has moved on.
- How Money Works (W6xkZy9nkaI): the longest hold (32.5 s) is stock under the sponsor; a flag per country cuts at 0.5 to 1 s.

## The numbers

Fireship mean shot 3.7 s against holds of 20 s plus; Casually mean 4.8 s against a 14.5 s pi symbol; the cut rate is not uniform in any of the three.

## The effect

The viewer gets time where there is something to read and none where there is not. A diagram held while the voice explains it twice (abstract, then "for example") lets the second explanation land on a picture the eye already knows.

## Counter-example

Maarek holds 75 s on one slide because the slide is building; AWS holds 257 s on a recording because the cursor is moving. A long hold with nothing moving and nothing to read is the still-run problem the craft notes measure (C-PACE-7).

## What it needs

A hold rule per scene kind: evidence and diagrams held for the whole passage that uses them, with a build or a camera move inside the hold; cards and reactions short.
