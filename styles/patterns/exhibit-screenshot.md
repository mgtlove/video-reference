# The exhibit screenshot

Group: word-and-picture. Say it like: "show the screenshot as evidence", "the post on screen", "rotated screenshot with a shadow", "make it look like an exhibit".

## Observed

- Fireship (I_KVMFrUtPk): articles, posts, documents and figures are shown as screenshots, slightly rotated with a shadow so they read as objects placed on the desk rather than as the page itself; shown whole, then pushed into the highlighted line.
- How Money Works (W6xkZy9nkaI): section openers show a clipping on a cutting mat with a headline that poses the section's question (0:29, 2:38, 4:47); the channel's own device, repeated.

## The numbers

Fireship: 24 evidence shots in 82, one accent colour per graphic. How Money Works: 3 clipping openers, each a hold of about 15 s under drift.

## The effect

An exhibit is something the narrator found, not something the narrator made, so it carries the authority of the outside world. The rotation and shadow say "object," which is why the same screenshot flat on the frame reads as a slide.

## Counter-example

AWS shows only the product; IBM shows only the board; Ken Burns shows real artefacts photographed, never screenshots. An exhibit of another person's post is their words on your frame, which the comedians use and the teachers never do.

## What it needs

An evidence still with an id and a frame treatment (rotation, shadow, mat) as a token, plus the push-in from `highlighter-on-the-cited-line`.
