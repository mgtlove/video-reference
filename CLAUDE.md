# video-reference: rules for an agent working here

- Read `catalog.md` before studying anything; it may already be here, measured.
- Record a source in `licences.md` before anything else, with the exact version or commit. Unclear licence: notes and links only.
- Nothing from a studied video is ever copied: no frame, clip, thumbnail, logo or caption text. Describe, measure, credit.
- Every number says where it came from (a shot log, an audio note, a standard) or says it is an estimate.
- A pattern needs a counter-example; a move with none is a rule, and rules live in `craft/`.
- This repo says what was observed. What to do with it lives in `video-kit` as data and defaults, with a verdict there, not here.
- Delete nothing; move aside and say so. Plain language, no em dashes.
